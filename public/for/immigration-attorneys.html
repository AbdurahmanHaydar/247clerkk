What it costs you now

The clock started before they messaged

A rejection letter dated three weeks ago and an enquiry sent tonight are a very different matter from the same enquiry sent the day the letter arrived. You need the date before you need anything else.

Time zones and languages

A client in Dubai, a family in Harare, a student in Berlin. None of them are messaging between eight and five, and not all of them are messaging in English.

Distress makes for vague enquiries

"They rejected my visa, can you help" contains almost nothing you can act on. Getting to the section and the date takes patient questions, at 23:00, every time.

What your clerk establishes before you read a word

  • Visa or permit type — spousal, critical skills, business, study, relative's, permanent residence — because the work is entirely different.
  • The date on the letter — the single most important fact in an immigration enquiry, and the one clients volunteer last.
  • Section of refusal — 8(4) or 8(6), so the appeal route is clear before the consultation starts.
  • Current status in the country — whether they are here legally, overstayed, or declared undesirable.
  • Dependants affected — spouse and children on the same application change both urgency and fee.
  • Language preference — so the conversation happens in the language the client is most precise in.

Questions

Does it speak languages other than English?

Yes. The clerk handles the intake conversation in the language the client opens in, which matters in a practice where clients are frequently more precise in Portuguese, French, Shona or isiZulu than in English. The summary reaches you in English.

Can it advise on whether an appeal will succeed?

No, and it is built to refuse. It establishes the facts an attorney needs — refusal section, dates, status, dependants — and tells the client plainly that an admitted practitioner will advise on prospects. Distressed clients ask this constantly, which is exactly why the boundary has to be built in rather than hoped for.

How does it handle urgent deadlines?

You define what urgent means for your practice — typically an appeal window under a certain number of working days. When intake establishes a date that triggers it, the enquiry is escalated and flagged rather than filed with the rest of the night's messages.
