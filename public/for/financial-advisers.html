What it costs you now

Discovery meetings that discover nothing

An hour spent establishing that a prospect has R40 000 to invest and wanted a general chat about markets is an hour that cost you a real client.

Referrals arrive without context

"My colleague said to speak to you" tells you nothing about assets, mandate or what they actually need until you are sitting in front of them.

The regulated line is easy to cross

A prospect asking whether they should move their retirement annuity wants advice, and any answer that is not "a licensed adviser will take you through that" is a problem.

What your clerk establishes before you read a word

  • Approximate assets under discussion — asked as a range, which prospects answer far more readily than an exact figure.
  • What they actually need — retirement transition, estate planning, investment mandate, risk cover, or a second opinion.
  • Mandate type — discretionary, advisory or a once-off plan — each is a different conversation and a different adviser.
  • Timeline — retiring in March is urgent; thinking about it eventually is a newsletter subscriber.
  • Where they came from — referral, seminar, search — so you know which channel produces clients rather than meetings.
  • Existing advisers — because a prospect with an incumbent is a different conversation to one starting fresh.

Questions

Will it give financial advice?

No, and this matters more in your practice than in most. Financial advice is regulated work. The clerk introduces itself as the practice's assistant, answers process and availability questions, establishes what the prospect needs, and states plainly that a licensed adviser will advise. It is built to hand over early.

Can it ask about assets without putting people off?

It asks for a broad range rather than an exact figure, and only after establishing what the person needs — which is the order that works. Prospects answer a range readily by message, and considerably more readily than they would to a stranger on a first phone call.

What happens to enquiries that are not a fit?

They are answered courteously, given a straight explanation of the practice's minimum where you want one, and filed with the reason. You get a record rather than a gap, which also means you can see how much of your enquiry flow is below the line you actually serve.
